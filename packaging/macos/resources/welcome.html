Audiosphere @VERSION@

Wavetable synthesizer — Hip Hop Edition

This installer sets up everything for you:

  • VST3 plug-in → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • Audio Unit plug-in → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  • Standalone app → /Applications

It includes 82 factory presets: 808s, basses, keys, pads, leads, plucks, bells, strings, lo-fi and FX.

Close your DAW before continuing. Click Customize on the Installation Type page to choose which formats to install.
