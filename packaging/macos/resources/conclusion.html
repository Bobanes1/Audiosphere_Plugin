Audiosphere is installed

Ableton Live 12

  1. Open Live > Settings > Plug-Ins.
  2. Switch on Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders (or Use Audio Units v2).
  3. Click Rescan. Audiosphere appears under Plug-Ins in the browser.

Logic Pro / GarageBand: pick Audiosphere from the Instrument slot under AU Instruments > Audiosphere.

Your own presets are saved to Documents/Audiosphere/Presets.
