# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-18
- Favourites: like presets with the star next to the preset name; liked presets are listed under
  **★ Favourites** in the preset menu.
- Built-in updater: notifies you about new releases, downloads the installer for your system,
  verifies it against the release's SHA256 checksums and starts it.
- 71 new factory presets (153 in total) across all categories.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-18
- First release: wavetable synth with 2 oscillators, sub and noise, filter, 3 envelopes, 2 LFOs,
  8-slot mod matrix, macros, effects and 82 factory presets.
- Windows installer and Linux package.
